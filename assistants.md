# Digital Employees

Digital Employees are pre-configured AI assistants for specific tasks.

## Viewing employees

1. Click the settings gear and select **Digital Employee**
2. View the list of available digital employees

![Digital Employees](/screenshots/en/assistants-list.png)

## Adding a new employee

Click the **+** button to add a new employee. Fill in the name, description, model, and instructions.


![Add Employee](/screenshots/en/assistants-add.png)
