# Digital Employees

Digital Employees are pre-configured AI assistants for specific tasks.

## Viewing employees

1. Click the settings gear and select **Employee**
2. View the list of available digital employees

![Digital Employees](/screenshots/assistants-list.png)

## Adding a new employee

Click the **+** or **Add** button to create a new digital employee. Configure its behavior, permissions, and skills.

![Add Employee](/screenshots/assistants-add.png)
