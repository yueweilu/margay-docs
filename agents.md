# AI Assistants

Margay supports multiple AI assistants. View and manage them in Settings.

## Viewing assistants

1. Click the settings gear at the bottom of the sidebar
2. Select **Agents** from the settings menu
3. You will see a list of configured AI assistants

![Agent settings](/screenshots/agent-selector.png)

## Supported assistants

Margay supports Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Codex, Kimi, and more. Each assistant can be configured individually.
